Harness assembly

Each playfield button needs one 3835 switch pair and one 3838 lamp pair. Remove their 2-pin housings and crimp fresh SXH contacts into one XHP-4: 1 lamp+, 2 lamp−, 3 NO, 4 COM. Preserve four separate conductors. Do not connect +12 V to an input. Insulate unused NC switch contacts. Housing cavity numbers must match the PCB header; check with a continuity meter before plugging in.

3835/3838 compatibility is confirmed by Adafruit for 1194 only. The EG STARTS menu-button terminal sizes remain unverified. Wire, splice insulation, cabinet strain relief and a suitable XH crimp tool depend on workshop setup; no unspecified terminal is presented as a verified fit. Panel, adapter and cabinet hardware remain BOM-provided items rather than newly selected substitutes.
